Books by Brandyn Knight

Two published books, one about carrying what cannot be named, and one about finding work that fits an actual life.

The Measure of a Soul

Collected Poems of Healing, Heritage, and Wayfinding.

A quiet book for a loud world. These poems come from grief, from family and place, and from the long work of returning to yourself. They do not arrive at triumph and they do not offer closure. What they offer is honesty, and the company of someone who has carried a difficult thing and kept walking.

For readers who have lost someone, for readers who are finding their way back, and for anyone who has needed a marker more than an answer.

Buy the paperback on Amazon

Independently published, 2026. ISBN 979-8-17269-993-1.

Ignite Your Career

Fueling Success with Authentic Passion.

A direct guide to career development that begins with a person rather than a job market. It treats a career as something built from what someone actually cares about, rather than from a template handed to them at the start.

Short by design. Written for someone standing at a career crossroad and wanting a clear first step rather than a theory of everything.

Free instruments

Two interactive tools are published and free to use. Both are built to help a person look at their own situation rather than be told what to do about it.

The Situational Cartography Laboratory. A structured way to map what is actually happening around a decision or a turning point: the facts, the pressures, the relationships, and the possibilities.

Open the laboratory

Career Discovery and Occupational Cartography. A career exploration instrument covering 52 Ontario occupations, built to compare real options against a person's own priorities.

Open career discovery

These books and tools are educational and reflective. They are not therapy, counselling, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis, contact 9-8-8, the Suicide Crisis Helpline, in Canada, or your local emergency services.
